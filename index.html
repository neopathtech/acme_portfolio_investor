Investment Thesis

A defensible moat in an underserved regional healthcare market

Northeast India faces a growing reproductive health and infertility burden, yet patients have historically been forced to travel to Delhi, Kolkata, or Guwahati. ACME captures this captive demand through accredited infrastructure, local cultural trust, and clinical excellence.

01

High Barrier-to-Entry Infrastructure

Commissioning compliant ART cleanrooms, laminar-flow theatres, and cryogenic tanks requires complex capital and clinical licensing under the ART Act 2021. ACME possesses fully operational, compliant infrastructure with zero ramp-up lag.

02

High-Margin Recurring Revenue Streams

Unlike single-treatment clinics, our revenue is balanced across IVF cycles, annual cryopreservation storage annuities, high-margin keyhole laparoscopic procedures, obstetric deliveries, neonatal ICU care, and OPD consultations.

03

Physician-Led Institutional Brand

Led by Dr. James Elangbam, one of the state's most sought-after endoscopic gynaecologists and reproductive physicians, creating immense patient loyalty, organic word-of-mouth patient referrals, and strong clinical credibility.

Growth Strategy

Capital allocation & regional scale roadmap

Deploying expansion capital into high-margin diagnostic insourcing and regional hub-and-spoke satellite outpatient clinics.

Phase 1 • 2026

In-House Genetics & PGT-A

Commissioning next-generation sequencing (NGS) for pre-implantation genetic testing and non-invasive prenatal screening (NIPS), capturing high-margin genetic testing revenues currently sent out-of-state.

Projected Margin Accretion: +18%
Phase 2 • 2027

Regional Satellite Spoke Network

Opening low-capex outpatient consultation & ultrasound monitoring hubs in Churachandpur, Senapati, and Kohima (Nagaland) to funnel high-ticket IVF and laparoscopic surgeries directly into the Imphal hospital.

Target Cycle Uplift: +65% Volume
Phase 3 • 2028

Aesthetic Gynaecology & Pelvic Health

Launching a dedicated cosmetic gynaecology, pelvic floor reconstruction, and laser rejuvenation wing targeting high-paying private pay and medical tourism demographics across Northeast India.

Target Payor Mix: 100% Cash / Private
Phase 4 • 2029-30

Endoscopy & ART Training Academy

Establishing a formal academic fellowship in collaboration with international endoscopic societies to train regional doctors, simultaneously generating tuition revenue and securing junior clinical talent.

Strategic Talent Moat • Institutional Prestige
